# Plan 1.1: Core Navigation & Hero

## Objective
Refactor the Navbar, HeroVideo, and Preloader components for mobile responsiveness. This ensures the immediate first impression of the site scales gracefully to small screens, maintaining the brutalist typography and touch targets without breaking GSAP animations.

## Context
- .gsd/SPEC.md
- src/components/Navbar.tsx
- src/components/HeroVideo.tsx
- src/components/Preloader.tsx

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
